    A Home that knows you

    Tap a mood and a queue starts, your favourites first. Up to four daily mixes grown from the songs you love, new releases from artists you follow, and shelves ranked by what you actually play.

    Daily Mix 1Your rotation, warmed up
    Daily Mix 2Late-night favourites
    Daily Mix 3Deep cuts and B-sides

    Colour from every cover

    The glass, the glow and the controls take their colour from the album art, over true AMOLED black.

    Lines that light up right on the beat, word by word, as the song plays

    Lyrics that keep time

    Synced lyrics from several sources, line by line and word by word.

    Real offline downloads

    Songs, albums and whole playlists on the device. No connection, no expiry.

    Mix, EQ and sleep timer

    DJ-style transitions between tracks, crossfade, a built-in equaliser, and a timer that waits for the song to end.

    Ambient mode

    Dock it and the screen becomes an aurora lit by the album art, with a big clock and an OLED-safe drift.

    Share playlists by QR

    A playlist becomes a code another phone can scan. The whole list travels in the code; no server, no link rot.

    Watch and car

    A Wear OS app with its own screens, voice search and lyrics, plus Android Auto, a widget and a Quick Settings tile.

    Make it yours

    Light, dark or true black, Material You colour from your wallpaper, or an accent of your own.

    On Android

    Installed in about a minute

    Hymn isn't on the Play Store, so Android asks before installing it. That prompt is normal for any app from outside the store.

    1. Download the APK

      Tap download above. Your browser may warn about the file type; it says that for every APK.

    2. Allow the install

      Open the file. Android offers Install unknown apps; turn it on for your browser, then come back.

    3. Open and play

      Tap Install, then Open. Nothing to sign up for; it works the moment it starts.

    Is it safe to install from here?

    Downloads come straight from the project's GitHub releases, so you can always check the file at the source. Hymn needs Android 7.0 or newer.

    How do I put it on my Wear OS watch?

    The watch app needs Hymn on your phone and talks only to the phone build from the same release, so update both together. Watches have no browser: install it from a computer with adb install over Wi‑Fi debugging (Settings → Developer options on the watch), or with an app like Bugjaeger or Wear Installer on your phone.

    Windows or Mac warns about the installer

    On Windows the easiest way is the Microsoft Store (or the winget command): it's signed and updates itself. The direct installers aren't code-signed yet. If SmartScreen steps in, choose More info → Run anyway. On a Mac, the one-line Terminal command installs it with no prompt; if you downloaded the DMG, open the app once, then allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
